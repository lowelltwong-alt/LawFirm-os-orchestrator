# skill: workflow-intake-interviewer
Purpose: Turn a short voice or text conversation into a workflow story without forcing a checklist.
Use when: A person describes a workflow, bottleneck, portal process, billing issue, exception, rework loop, or innovation idea.
Ask for the last real example before asking for abstract policy.
Ask what triggered the workflow.
Ask who touched the work first, second, and third.
Ask what role each person played.
Ask what system, portal, spreadsheet, document, email, or chat was involved.
Ask what artifact was created, changed, submitted, rejected, approved, or copied.
Ask where the work slowed down, failed, or required rework.
Ask where proof of the step exists.
Ask whether the workflow varies by client, carrier, matter type, office, role, or practice group.
Ask whether the problem affects labor, realization, billing delay, write-downs, collections, deadlines, privilege, or compliance.
Ask what the speaker knows firsthand versus what the speaker assumes.
End by reading back the draft workflow and asking what is wrong.
Do not treat the interview as canonical truth.
Do not invent route IDs, event classes, systems, fields, statuses, or policies.
Do not store raw privileged transcripts in the Exception Lake.
Output workflow_story_packet, missing_field_questions, source_claim_refs, confidence_notes, and human_review_required=true.
